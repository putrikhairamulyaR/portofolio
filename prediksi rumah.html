House Price Analysis & Prediction

Analyzed property characteristics to identify the key drivers of house prices and develop a data-driven approach to price prediction.

📊 Project Snapshot

🏠 1,000 Property Records
⚙️ 8 Features
✅ 0 Missing Values

⚠️ The Problem

House prices vary across properties, but the factors behind these differences are not always obvious. Property characteristics such as square footage, neighborhood quality, and lot size may contribute differently to the final valuation.

The challenge was to identify which characteristics have the greatest influence on house prices and determine whether these patterns could be used to produce reliable price estimates.

🎯 Objective

The project was designed to answer the following questions:

  • Which property characteristics have the strongest influence on house prices?
  • Does property size have a greater influence on price than other property characteristics?
  • How well can these characteristics explain differences in house prices?
  • Which machine learning model can provide the most accurate house price predictions?

🔎 Approach & Analysis

1. Data Quality Analysis
Validated 1,000 property records across 8 features and checked for missing values, duplicates, and data consistency.

2. Feature Distribution Analysis
Examined the distribution of property characteristics to understand the composition and variation within the dataset.

3. Price Driver Analysis
Analyzed the relationship between property characteristics and house prices to identify the factors with the strongest impact on price variation.

4. Correlation Analysis
Measured relationships between numerical features and house prices to identify the strongest pricing signals.

5. Comparative Analysis
Compared property characteristics across different groups to understand how changes in property attributes correspond to differences in house prices.

6. Predictive Modeling
Compared Linear Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest models using MAE, RMSE, and R² to determine the most effective approach for house price prediction.

📈 Results & Insights

We compared three machine learning algorithms to predict housing prices: Linear Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest. The evaluation showed that factors such as Square Footage, Neighborhood Quality, and Lot Size had the most significant impact on the final valuation.

💡 Key Insights

Square Footage Property size was identified as one of the strongest factors associated with house price variation.
Neighborhood Quality Neighborhood quality also showed a significant contribution to differences in property valuation.
Lot Size Lot size contributed to the variation in final property valuation and provided an additional pricing signal.
Model Performance Linear Regression outperformed Decision Tree and Random Forest based on MAE, RMSE, and R².
Main Insight

Property Characteristics Can Provide Strong Pricing Signals

The results show that house prices can be effectively estimated from property characteristics, with Square Footage, Neighborhood Quality, and Lot Size providing important signals for valuation.

💭 Why It Matters

House price estimation should not rely solely on intuition or subjective judgment. Understanding which property characteristics contribute most to price provides a more data-driven basis for evaluating property value.

The model evaluation also helps determine which approach can translate these relationships into more accurate price estimates.

🚀 Impact

From Property Data to Price Estimation

The project transforms property data into actionable insights about what influences house prices, particularly Square Footage, Neighborhood Quality, and Lot Size.

These insights provide a data-driven basis to estimate and predict house prices based on the characteristics of a property, helping users get a reasonable indication of how much a property may be worth.

With the best-performing model achieving an R² of 0.9984, the developed approach demonstrates strong predictive performance on the evaluated dataset and can serve as a foundation for an interactive house price estimation application.
